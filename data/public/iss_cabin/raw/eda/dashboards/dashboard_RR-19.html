Environmental Data App

The Environmental Data App (EDA) is a portal where users can visualize and compare International Space Station (ISS) cabin, hardware environmental telemetry data, and radiation data gathered from spaceflight missions. On the Mission Dashboard the users can visualize temperature, carbon dioxide (CO2), and relative humidity measurements and radiation doses recorded in the ISS cabin and ground control for the duration of the mission. In addition to Temperature and Relative Humidity data collected from both flight and ground hardware units. The Mission Comparison feature can be used to visualize data across multiple missions. Raw and summary data are available for download on the Data Tables tab through the Data Tables section.


Rodent Research 19 (Mighty Mice in Space)

The Rodent Research 19 (RR-19) investigation entitled "Mighty Mice in Space: Pre-clinical Evaluation of a Broad Spectrum Myostatin Inhibitor to Prevent Muscle and Bone Loss Due to Disuse project" explores the efficacy of inhibiting myostatin/activin signaling in mice in a microgravity environment in order to preserve their muscle and/or bone mass, or to enhance the recovery of their skeletal muscle and bone mass after their return to Earth. It is hoped that the results of this research will yield novel treatment options for muscle and bone loss in humans, in the contexts of spaceflight as well as various pathologies on Earth. RR-19 will launch 40 female mice (C57BL/6J, 8 MSTN -/- knock-out, 32 wild type, aged 10-12 weeks) on SpaceX CRS-19, and begin ground control studies with the same number and type of research subjects on a three-day delay. The total compliment of 40 in flight mice will be transported, live, back to Earth.

For this release, the ground dates have been matched to the flight dates.
